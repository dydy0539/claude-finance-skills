# Charter Communications (CHTR) — Company-Specific Module

Idiosyncratic content for Charter. Universal cable-led KPIs are covered in `modules/cable-led.md` — do **not** repeat those here. This module covers only what's distinctive to Charter's segments, named programs, corporate actions, and stated strategic narrative.

---

## Segment Structure (Reporting Quirks)

Charter reports as **a single consolidated business** (no formal segments). Sub-line splits in supplementals:

- **Residential** — Internet, Mobile, Video, Voice
- **Spectrum Business (SMB)** — Internet, mobile, video, voice for small/mid businesses
- **Spectrum Enterprise** — connectivity, networking, managed services for mid-market and large enterprise
- **Advertising sales** — Spectrum Reach
- **Mobile** — broken out separately as a growth product line

Charter is **pure-play cable connectivity** — no media, no parks, no content production. This makes Charter the **cleanest read** on cable connectivity economics in the public markets.

## Idiosyncratic Programs & Assets

### Spectrum Mobile (MVNO on Verizon)
Launched 2018. Runs on Verizon's network with WiFi + CBRS offload. **Spectrum Mobile has been the fastest-growing wireless line product among the five US providers** (CMCSA, CHTR, T, VZ, TMUS). Track:
- **Mobile lines** total + net adds for the period
- **Mobile attach rate** to Internet (broadband)
- **Anytime Upgrade** product / unlimited offerings — pricing aggressiveness drives growth
- **CBRS deployment** progress for offload economics

Charter's mobile growth is a meaningful share-shift indicator vs. Big 3.

### Rural Buildouts (RDOF + BEAD)
Charter has been **aggressive on rural footprint expansion** — distinct from Comcast, which focuses on existing urban/suburban footprint. Funded partly by:
- **RDOF** (Rural Digital Opportunity Fund — FCC subsidy program, Charter won ~$1.2B over 10 years in 2020 auction)
- **BEAD** (Broadband Equity, Access, and Deployment — $42.5B federal program, allocations 2024–2025)

Track:
- **New passings added** in the period (rural buildouts)
- **Rural penetration** ramp
- **Subsidy capex offset** (some capex is partly subsidized)

This is a **structural growth lever** unique to Charter's strategy — Comcast doesn't have a comparable rural buildout program.

### Liberty Broadband / John Malone Influence
**Liberty Broadband owns ~25%** of Charter (verify current ownership). John Malone–affiliated. Liberty Broadband transactions occasionally affect float, governance, and capital allocation. Watch for:
- Any Liberty / Charter merger or simplification announcements
- Buyback dynamics (Charter buys back Liberty stock to maintain ratio)

A Liberty Broadband / Charter combination has been a recurring topic — verify status from current docs.

### Programming Carriage Disputes
Charter has been **the most aggressive cable operator** in pushing back on programmer pricing. Notable past dispute: **Disney 2023** carriage agreement that included Disney+ value bundle. Charter's stance: video is being repositioned, not defended. Track:
- Any active disputes with programmers
- Programming cost dynamics
- Video product positioning ("seamless entertainment" bundle approach)

### DirecTV / Dish Video Distribution Discussions
Charter has been involved in **video distribution restructuring discussions** — historically explored combinations with DirecTV / Dish in pay-TV. Verify status from current docs.

### Capital Structure: No Dividend, Aggressive Buybacks
Charter pays **no dividend** — capital return is **100% buyback**. Among the **most buyback-aggressive** S&P 500 companies historically. Track:
- **Total $ buyback** in the period
- **Net share count reduction** YoY
- **Authorization remaining**
- Buyback pace as function of FCF and leverage capacity

### Leverage Profile
Charter operates at **~4–4.5x net debt / EBITDA** by design — significantly higher than Comcast (~2.5x). This is intentional capital-structure efficiency. Track:
- **Net debt / EBITDA** vs. stated target band
- Interest expense exposure
- Debt maturity wall and refinancing activity (especially in higher-rate environments)
