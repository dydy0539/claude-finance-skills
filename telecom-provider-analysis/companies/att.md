# AT&T (T) — Company-Specific Module

## Segment Structure (Reporting Quirks)

AT&T reports four segments — be precise about which segment each metric belongs to:

- **Mobility** — Wireless services + equipment. Includes consumer, business, and FirstNet wireless. The largest and most profitable segment. Postpaid phone metrics live here.
- **Consumer Wireline** — AT&T Fiber + legacy copper DSL/voice for consumer. The fiber subscriber ramp is the bull-thesis core.
- **Business Wireline** — Enterprise + government connectivity (excluding wireless, which is in Mobility). Structurally declining ~mid-single digits YoY but cash-generative; pace of decline stabilization is a watch-item.
- **Latin America** — Mexico wireless operations (DTV LatAm was divested 2022). Reported in Mexican pesos; FX-distorted growth — flag currency impact.

When extracting consolidated wireless metrics, note: Mobility includes FirstNet net adds and equipment. Some carriers separate these; AT&T does not.

## Idiosyncratic Programs & Assets

### FirstNet
Government / public safety dedicated network — AT&T's structural moat in postpaid wireless. A 25-year contract with the federal First Responder Network Authority. Track:
- FirstNet **subscriber count** (cumulative connections)
- Any **agency wins** or buildout milestones disclosed
- FirstNet's contribution to Mobility postpaid net adds (when broken out)

This is unique to AT&T — no other Big 3 carrier has a comparable government anchor.

### AT&T Fiber Buildout
Specific stated target: **~30M fiber passings** (verify exact number in current docs — has shifted with deal activity). Track:
- **Cumulative passings** vs. target
- **Pace of new passings** added in the period
- **Penetration rate** = fiber subs ÷ fiber passings (AT&T discloses; the curve trajectory is closely watched — penetration typically accelerates 12–24 months post-passing)

### AT&T Internet Air (FWA)
AT&T's FWA product is **smaller and more selective** than Verizon/T-Mobile FWA — positioned as a fiber-fill product in non-fiber-passed markets. Don't anchor the snapshot on FWA for AT&T; note progress but it's secondary.

### DirecTV Residual Stake
AT&T retained a ~70% economic stake when DirecTV was carved out to TPG in 2021. **Monetization or sale of this stake** is a recurring corporate action item — track for any updates. Equity-method earnings from DirecTV flow through "other" line items.

### WarnerMedia Spinoff (Historical Context Only)
WarnerMedia was spun off and merged with Discovery in April 2022 to form Warner Bros. Discovery. AT&T retains no operating exposure. Mention only if the docs reference it for comparability.

## Stated Strategic Thesis (Three Pillars)

AT&T's investment narrative — use these as the three "Where They Stand" bullets:

1. **Fiber ramp** — AT&T Fiber subscriber engine still firing? Passings on track? Penetration accelerating?
2. **Wireless defense** — holding postpaid share and ARPU vs. T-Mobile, Verizon, and cable MVNOs? Churn improving?
3. **Deleveraging** — net debt declining toward stated **2.5x** net debt / EBITDA target? FCF guidance intact? Dividend coverage comfortable?

## Idiosyncratic Watch-List

Items unique to AT&T that don't appear in the universal wireless-led module:

- **Net debt vs. 2.5x target** — AT&T's deleveraging trajectory is more thesis-central than for Verizon or T-Mobile because the post-divestiture balance sheet is still elevated
- **Mexico Mobility** — segment revenue / EBITDA / subs; FX impact on consolidated growth
- **Business Wireline decline pace** — is the secular ~mid-single-digit decline stabilizing or accelerating? Any large enterprise contract wins / losses?
- **Annual dividend** — currently $1.11/share (verify); FCF dividend coverage cushion
- **Capex post-fiber-peak** — management has guided lower capex post fiber buildout; track trajectory
