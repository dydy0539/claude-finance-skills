## Segment Structure (Reporting Quirks)

Comcast restructured its segments in **2024** (effective Q1 2024 reporting). Verify current structure against the documents — but as of the restructure:

- **Connectivity & Platforms** (the connectivity focus of this skill)
  - **Residential Connectivity** — Domestic broadband, video, voice, Xfinity Mobile
  - **Business Services Connectivity** — SMB + Enterprise connectivity
  - **Sky** — UK / Italy / Germany broadband + video + OTT
  - **Media** (within Connectivity & Platforms) — advertising, content licensing, NBC/Telemundo broadcast networks (note: **NOT** the cable nets, which are in Content & Experiences)

- **Content & Experiences** (largely out of scope for this skill — context only)
  - **Media** — NBCUniversal cable networks, Peacock streaming, content studios
  - **Studios** — Universal Pictures, DreamWorks Animation, Sky Studios
  - **Theme Parks** — Universal Studios properties (Orlando, Hollywood, Beijing, Singapore + Epic Universe opening 2025)

The skill's focus is **Connectivity & Platforms**. Use the C&P revenue, EBITDA, and FCF figures for connectivity comparisons — **not** consolidated, which is distorted by NBCU and parks.

## Idiosyncratic Programs & Assets

### Sky (International Connectivity + Media)
Acquired 2018 for ~$39B. Operates in UK, Italy, and Germany. Mix of:
- Broadband + video distribution (the connectivity contribution)
- Original content production (the media contribution)
- Sports rights (Premier League etc. — material cost item)

Sky's broadband/video dynamics differ from US — separate competitive context. **Flag Sky's contribution if material to the period's results**, but note that Sky's economics aren't comparable to the US Big 3 / Charter setup.

### Xfinity Mobile (MVNO on Verizon)
Comcast's mobile product, launched 2017. Runs on Verizon's network with WiFi + CBRS offload. **Xfinity Mobile lines** are the relevant subscriber metric (not "postpaid phone net adds"). Track:
- Mobile lines net adds + cumulative
- Mobile attach rate to Internet
- CBRS / offload deployment progress

Comcast has been a meaningful share-of-postpaid taker against the Big 3 — sum Comcast mobile lines with Charter mobile lines for the cable share-of-wireless story.

### Network Upgrade — DOCSIS 4.0 / Mid-Split / High-Split
Comcast is deploying:
- **Mid-split / high-split DOCSIS 3.1** — interim symmetrical multi-gig
- **DOCSIS 4.0** — long-term competitive answer to FTTP
- Selective **fiber-to-the-home overbuild** in highest-competition markets

Track deployment progress, % of footprint upgraded, and capex trajectory.

### Peacock Streaming
NBCUniversal's streaming service. **Subscriber count and EBITDA losses** disclosed periodically. Peacock has been a structural drag on consolidated EBITDA but losses have narrowed. **Mention as one paragraph in non-connectivity context** — do not anchor the snapshot on Peacock.

### Versant Spinoff (Announced 2024 — Verify Status)
Comcast announced in November 2024 plans to **spin off most of NBCU's cable networks** (USA, CNBC, MSNBC, Oxygen, E!, SyFy, Golf Channel) into a new publicly traded company tentatively called **"Versant"**. Excludes NBC broadcast network, Bravo, Peacock, and the broadcast/streaming-aligned networks. Verify status:
- If pre-close: spin timing and pro forma capital structure expectations
- If post-close: how Comcast's reporting structure changes

### Universal Studios — Epic Universe (2025)
Major new theme park opening Orlando in 2025. Capex peak associated with this buildout. **Mention in non-connectivity context only** — material to consolidated capex but not to connectivity story.

### Hulu Stake (Historical)
Comcast sold its 33% Hulu stake to Disney in mid-2024 for ~$8.6B. Generates a one-time inflow / capital return / debt paydown event in 2024 financials — mention if material to the reporting period.
