# Telecom Industry Module

Append these sections after the core earnings analysis Bottom Line when analyzing any telecom company.

---

## T1. Subscriber Metrics

The most market-sensitive section for telecom earnings.

- **Postpaid phone net adds** — absolute number and YoY trend; is the company gaining or losing share?
- **Postpaid phone churn** — report as %; below 1% is best-in-class; rising churn is a red flag
- **Prepaid net adds / churn** — directionally important; less monetizable than postpaid
- **Total wireless net adds** — include tablets, wearables, IoT if broken out
- Compare net adds to the prior quarter and prior year quarter; note any sequential inflection

---

## T2. ARPU & Revenue Quality

- **Postpaid phone ARPU** — YoY change; is pricing power holding?
- **Service revenue** — strip out equipment revenue to isolate recurring stream; report YoY growth
- **Equipment revenue** — note if upgrade cycle is boosting or depressing topline optics
- Comment on promotional intensity: is ARPU growth coming from pricing or mix, or being eroded by promos?

---

## T3. EBITDA Service Margin

- Report **EBITDA service margin** = EBITDA ÷ service revenue (not total revenue)
- This is the industry-standard profitability measure for wireless; total revenue margin is distorted by equipment
- YoY change and trend direction
- Note any one-time items (restructuring, network costs, lease accounting) that affect comparability

---

## T4. Network Capex & Capex Intensity

- **Total capex** and **capex as % of revenue** (capex intensity)
- Is capex rising (investment cycle) or declining (harvest mode)?
- Break out **wireless capex** vs. **wireline/fiber capex** if disclosed
- Management commentary on capex guidance and trajectory — telecom capex cycles are multi-year

---

## T5. Free Cash Flow After Capex

- **FCF = Operating cash flow − capex**
- Report absolute FCF and FCF margin
- Is FCF covering the dividend? Report **FCF payout ratio** = dividends ÷ FCF
- Note any working capital swings, one-time payments (spectrum, litigation) that distort FCF
- YoY FCF trend and management's FCF guidance

---

## T6. Fixed Wireless Access (FWA)

- **FWA net adds** for the quarter and cumulative subscriber base
- Revenue contribution if disclosed
- Management's FWA subscriber target and progress vs. target
- Competitive positioning: is FWA cannibalizing cable broadband, or attracting new-to-broadband customers?
- ARPU for FWA vs. fiber (if available) — helps assess monetization quality

---

## T7. Broadband & Fiber

- **Fiber net adds** — absolute number and YoY trend
- **Total broadband net adds** (fiber + copper/DSL + cable if applicable)
- **Fiber passings** — cumulative homes/businesses passed; pace of buildout vs. target
- **Fiber penetration rate** = fiber subscribers ÷ fiber passings; rising penetration = maturing market
- **Fiber + mobile bundle attach rate** — convergence is a key strategic moat; rising attach = pricing power and churn reduction

---

## T8. 5G & Spectrum Deployment

- Coverage metrics: population coverage (C-band, mmWave, low-band) vs. prior quarter
- Network quality commentary: any third-party rankings (Opensignal, Ookla, RootMetrics) cited?
- Spectrum holdings update: any new licenses, auctions, or build-out milestones
- Standalone 5G (SA) vs. non-standalone (NSA) progress — SA enables network slicing / enterprise use cases
- Enterprise 5G pipeline: deals, private network wins if disclosed

---

## T9. Telecom Bottom Line

Synthesize the telecom-specific picture:
- Is subscriber momentum accelerating or decelerating?
- Is the company a share gainer or defender in wireless?
- Is the fiber/FWA buildout on track and converting to revenue?
- Is FCF sufficient to fund capex, dividends, and debt service simultaneously?
- What is the single biggest risk to the telecom thesis heading into next quarter?
